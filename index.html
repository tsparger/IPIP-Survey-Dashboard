Completion by region

Locations in

Two metrics: Submitted = the Region+Location+Department combo has at least one row in SharePoint, regardless of how the Confirmation was answered. Validated = at least one submission for that combo has the Confirmation answered Yes (for the Department Survey, either InfoCorrect or InfoModified equals 'Yes'; for the Pharmacy Survey, PharmacyDispenseValidated equals 'Yes'). The green check on each survey's dropdown only appears for validated entries. Each Region+Location+Department combo is counted once regardless of how many times it has been submitted.

Printer Survey: submitting already requires every printer field, so there is no separate validated metric — the second number counts printer sets (one row per bin-label printer) recorded against that region or location. Its denominator is the same pharmacy department location list the Pharmacy Survey uses.
